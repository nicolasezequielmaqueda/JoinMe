**Course:** Introduction to Mobile Systems — Mobile for Social Good

**Team:** Nicolás Maqueda and Jaime Criado. Both instructors have accepted repository access, as confirmed by the team.

**Platform:** Android, using approved Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Multiplatform. iOS is optional.

**Links:** [Repository](https://github.com/nicolasezequielmaqueda/JoinMe) · [Setup](DEPLOY.md) · [Supporting notes](docs/m1/M1-details.md)

## Project canvas

**User and problem.** Adult Erasmus and international students in a residence want to share activities but may not know who is interested and available. A newcomer may find a free table tennis table but nobody to play with. Football fans may struggle to gather enough players through word of mouth.

**Our solution.** Students can find, create and join activities with a time, shared place and participant limit. A phone lets them check activities around the residence. Planned reminders can help them remember their plans. The four screens are Activities, Activity Detail, Create Activity and My Activities.

**Main task:** Activities → Table Tennis → Detail → Join → `1/2` becomes `2/2 FULL` → My Activities.

## Evidence and assumptions

**[E] Evidence:** [Sawir et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1177/1028315307299699) report loneliness and isolation among international students in Australia. This gives background to the problem; it does not prove our app works. [WhatsApp groups](https://www.whatsapp.com/groups) already offer messages, polls, events and attendance responses. JoinMe focuses on an activity list, filters and available places.

**[A] Assumptions:** This list may make finding a game easier. Students may prefer joining an activity to asking strangers, and shared residence places may be enough. However, they may prefer their existing WhatsApp group to another app. We do not claim to solve loneliness or guarantee friendships.

## User stories and acceptance criteria

- **Discover:** As a student, I want to find activities to do with others. Cards show type, time, place, participant count and text status. Filters show matching activities or a clear empty message.
- **Join:** As a student, I want to take part without already knowing others. Join increases the count once, changes to Leave and adds the activity to My Activities. A full activity blocks further joins.
- **Create:** As a student, I want to invite others to an activity. The form checks required fields, a future start and a limit of 2–30 people. The activity appears in both lists with its creator counted as `1/N`.

**Targets to test:** join without help in under 60 seconds and at most five taps; create in under two minutes. **Outside scope:** chat, profiles/followers, photos, GPS, payments, ratings, AI matching, complex login, tracking and activity editing.

## Prototype and feedback

**M1:** working navigation and made-up data in memory; changes reset when the app process restarts. **Next:** Room to save data, Supabase to share it, loading/error messages and saved activities available offline. Planned reminders are optional; My Activities remains available if permission is denied.

**[E] Peer review:** Two classmates from another team and class session tried finding table tennis, joining and locating it in My Activities. Both finished quickly without help or observed difficulty. We kept the flow. We did not record exact times or taps; this small review does not prove general usability.

## Accessibility, risks and team work

**Requirements:** usable at maximum font size; statuses understandable from text, without relying on colour. The team reports that the maximum-font check passed on the Medium Phone API 36.1 emulator (22 October 2026). The team also confirmed that OPEN, FULL, JOINED, CANCELLED and COMPLETED and the main action labels are understandable from their text on the same emulator.

**Data and risks:** made-up data in M1; later, activity details, user IDs and participation in Room/Supabase. IDs and participation may reveal activity patterns, so we will minimise stored data. Use shared places, limit notes and discourage personal information. Offline data may be old; spam remains a limitation.

**Responsibilities:** Nicolás and Jaime worked together on the design, implementation, documentation and checks. Both share responsibility for the full main flow and the demonstration.
